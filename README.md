# E-commerce Sales Analysis 2025

An end-to-end data analysis project exploring e-commerce business performance using **Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib**.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 2025 e-commerce data to identify sales trends, evaluate product profitability, understand customer behavior, and investigate business performance issues.

The main goal is to transform raw data into actionable business insights and recommendations.

## Key Analysis

- Business KPIs: revenue, profit, average order value, and customer metrics.
- Monthly sales and profit trends.
- Product and category profitability.
- Revenue analysis by city and sales channel.
- Top 10 customers by revenue.
- Payment method issues and order cancellations.
- Delivery performance and customer ratings.
- Executive dashboard with key business insights.

## Business Conclusions & Recommendations

### 1. Sales Seasonality

December generated the highest monthly revenue (**532,250 UAH**), while February had the lowest (**168,220 UAH**). December revenue was approximately 3.16 times higher than February revenue.

**Recommendation:** Prepare inventory, marketing campaigns, and operational capacity in advance of the November–December peak season.

### 2. Product and Category Profitability

Electronics generated the highest category revenue (**1,143,600 UAH**) and gross profit (**412,680 UAH**), but had the lowest gross margin (**36.09%**). Home products achieved the highest margin (**52.49%**).

At the product level, Smartwatch generated the highest revenue (**483,000 UAH**), while Perfume generated the highest profit (**149,600 UAH**).

**Recommendation:** Review procurement costs and pricing in Electronics, while exploring opportunities to grow sales of high-margin categories such as Home and Clothing.

### 3. Payment Method Performance

Cash on delivery had the highest cancellation rate (**11.41%**) and return rate (**7.48%**). For Card payments, these rates were **5.57%** and **3.23%**, respectively.

**Recommendation:** Introduce additional order confirmation procedures and test incentives for customers choosing prepaid payment methods.

### 4. Delivery Performance and Customer Satisfaction

Orders delivered within 1–3 days received average ratings between **4.03 and 4.08**. For deliveries taking 4–5 days, ratings dropped to **3.44–3.46**.

Kyiv had the shortest average delivery time (**1.50 days**), while Dnipro had the longest (**3.05 days**).

**Recommendation:** Investigate logistics delays in slower regions, improve delivery processes, and aim to keep average delivery time within 3 days.

### 5. Geographic and Channel Performance

Kyiv generated the highest city revenue (**1,132,350 UAH**, or **32.13%** of total revenue). The Website was the leading sales channel, contributing **1,740,450 UAH** (**49.38%** of total revenue).

**Recommendation:** Maintain the Website as a key sales channel while exploring opportunities to increase revenue through the App and Instagram.

## Final Business Takeaway

The business generated **3.52 million UAH in revenue** and **1.63 million UAH in gross profit**, demonstrating a solid gross margin of **46.15%**.

The analysis identified three key improvement opportunities:

- Reduce cancellations and returns associated with Cash on delivery.
- Improve delivery speed, particularly in regions with longer delivery times.
- Increase profitability in Electronics by reviewing procurement costs and pricing.

These actions could help improve operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and overall business profitability.
